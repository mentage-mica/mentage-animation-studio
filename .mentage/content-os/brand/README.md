# 00_brand

Core brand assets for Mentage LLC: voice, language, and audience definitions.

## Purpose

Defines how Mentage sounds, what words it uses, who its audiences are, and what the products mean to those audiences. This is the source of truth for tone, language, and positioning in all content.

## What belongs here

- Founder voice guide for Jerin Zachariah
- Brand voice and tone guidelines
- Product language rules (safe and prohibited terms for MEVA and Mentage Elements)
- Audience persona documents
- Taglines and brand positioning statements

## What does not belong here

- LinkedIn post drafts (those go in 05_linkedin/post_drafts/)
- Compliance documents (those go in 01_compliance/)
- Product strategy or pricing (those go in 06_product/ and 04_pricing/)
- Finalized approved assets (those go in 07_outputs/)

## Public-safe or internal-only

Most brand files are internal-only. Taglines and public positioning statements may be public-safe once reviewed. Mark each file clearly.
